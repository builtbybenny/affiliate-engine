# 📈 SaaS Edition — 30-Day Playbook (no ad spend)

The SaaS audience = founders, freelancers, creators, marketers. They don't
buy products; they buy **saved time and tool stacks**. Your content formats
are already retooled for this (see `core/config.py` WEEKLY_CALENDAR).

## The conversion path (different from physical products)

```
Reel/Carousel (hook: stack, comparison, before/after)
   → Bio link page (free-trial badges, one tap per tool)
      → Vendor free trial (cookie set)
         → You earn recurring commission for months/years
```

Recurring math: 5 GoHighLevel referrals ≈ $195/mo recurring at 40% —
one good comparison reel can outearn 100 physical-product posts.

## Week-by-week

**Week 1 — Authority setup.** Post daily per calendar. Pin the "my full
stack" carousel. Bio: "The exact tools I use — start each free 👇".
Apply to Tier-1 programs with your live bio page as proof.

**Week 2 — Comparisons.** X vs Y carousels are the highest-intent format.
Use the `alt` column in products.csv. Never trash competitors — "different
budgets, different fits" keeps you credible.

**Week 3 — Proof.** Screen recordings (drop mp4 into
`data/screen_recordings/<tool-id>.mp4`) of real tool UI in the first 8s of
reels. This is your unfair advantage: 90% of affiliate pages never show the
actual product.

**Week 4 — Compound.** Re-render your top 2 posts with new hooks (same
product, new seed). Add UTM-tagged links. Kill bottom-3 hashtags in
data/hashtags.txt using scripts/insights.py data.

## Compliance for SaaS

- Free-trial claims must match the vendor's current offer (keep `trial`
  column honest; trials change often).
- Never promise income ("make $5k/mo") — platforms penalize income claims;
  say "replaces 5 subscriptions" instead.
- Disclosure stays in every caption (engine handles it).

## KPI targets (SaaS edition)

| Metric | Healthy | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Profile → bio-link tap | >3% | SaaS audiences click more than shoppers |
| Trial starts / 1000 reach | 2–5 | Track in vendor dashboards |
| Saves per carousel | >2% of reach | Stacks/lists get saved |
| Trial → paid (vendor side) | 15–40% | You don't control this; pick vendors that do |

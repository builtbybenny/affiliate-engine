Privacy Policy — Stack & Save

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Stack & Save is an independent content page that shares information about software free trials and deals. This policy explains how the automation behind our posts works and what data it handles.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, or share personal data from people who view our posts or visit this site. This website is static — it sets no cookies, runs no trackers, and stores nothing about you.

Social media activity

If you comment on or interact with our posts on Instagram or Facebook, those platforms handle your data under their own privacy policies. We only see the same public content any visitor can see, and we never share it further.

Automated publishing

Our own posts are prepared and published with official Meta Graph APIs using credentials that belong to the Stack & Save page only. These credentials never access private information of any other user or account.

Affiliate links

Some posts link to software products under affiliate arrangements. If you click through and start a trial, the vendor's own site and terms apply — we receive no personal data about you from those vendors.

Questions? Message @stackandsavehq on Instagram or Facebook.
